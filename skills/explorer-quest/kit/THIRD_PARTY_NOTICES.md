# Third-party notices

Parts of the engine are adapted from **lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar** (https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar), under the MIT licence:

| File | Adapted from |
|---|---|
| `retro.js` | cel-shading core, `limb()`, flare values (`styles/cel-anime-80s`: `cel.js`, `rider.js`, `fx.js`, `head80.js`) |
| `film.js` | WebGL2 post pass structure (`core/post/crt.js`); the CRT stages are replaced by 16 mm film stages (gate weave, halation, grade, light leak, flicker, scratches, dust, grain) |

The world map coastlines in `mapdata.js` are generated by `make_map.py` from **Natural Earth** 1:110m land polygons (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/), which are in the public domain. The source GeoJSON is downloaded at build time and is not redistributed here.

No upstream images, fonts, music, samples or voices are used. The adventure score and SFX are original and synthesised in `audio.js`; no film score, logo, font or likeness from any existing adventure franchise is used. The voiceover is generated with Microsoft Edge neural TTS through `edge-tts` (`make_voice.py`).
