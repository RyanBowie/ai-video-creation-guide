# Third-party notices

**Data:** GCAT (General Catalog of Artificial Space Objects) by Jonathan C. McDowell, https://planet4589.org/space/gcat, licensed under CC BY 4.0. `data.js` holds yearly counts derived from `launchlog.tsv` by `build_data.py`. The counts are orbital launch attempts, grouped by the launch vehicle's state.

**Style:** the look follows the "Data Storytelling" style prompt in **lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar** (https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar/blob/main/styles/dataviz/STYLE.md), MIT licence, © 2026 LemoLab. This engine was written from scratch in plain Canvas2D. It contains no lemo-opuscar code, images or other assets.

No third-party images, fonts, music, samples or voices are used. Text uses system fonts. The score and pencil/paper SFX are synthesised in `audio.js`. The voiceover is generated with Microsoft Edge neural TTS (`en-GB-RyanNeural`) through `edge-tts` (`make_voice.py`).

Falcon 9 is named only as a fact in the data. This is a community project, not affiliated with any launch provider or with Microsoft.
