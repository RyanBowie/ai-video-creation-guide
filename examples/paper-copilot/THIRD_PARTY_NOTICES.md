# Third-party notices

The paper-cutout look is inspired by **francozanardi/papermotion** (https://github.com/francozanardi/papermotion), MIT licence, which makes paper-cutout animated shorts entirely in code with AI agents. Paper Mario-style RPG games also influenced the look. This engine was written from scratch in plain Canvas2D. It contains no papermotion code, images or other assets.

No third-party images, fonts, music, samples or voices are used. Text uses system fonts. The score and paper SFX are synthesised in `audio.js`. The voiceover is generated with Microsoft Edge neural TTS (`en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural`) through `edge-tts` (`make_voice.py`).

Microsoft 365 Copilot and Clippy appear as affectionate fan references. This is a community project, not a Microsoft product.
